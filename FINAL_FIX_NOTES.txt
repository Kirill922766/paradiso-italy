PARADISO Italy — SAFE CATALOG FIX

1. This version never deletes the entire products table when saving a product.
2. Admin saves one product at a time. A stale browser tab cannot overwrite other products.
3. The API auto-repairs a catalog that consists only of blank “Новый товар” placeholders: first three rows become the original three products with their bundled photos; extra blank placeholders are hidden, not deleted, so order references remain safe.
4. Known original articles get their bundled image restored if the database lost the image field.
5. Bundled images are in public/images and are served by Cloudflare Assets.
6. IMPORTANT: after replacing the GitHub repository files, wait for Cloudflare deployment to finish, then hard-refresh the site (Ctrl+F5 on PC or clear site data on phone).
